**Category:** cooking
**Sources:** [[RAW/kitchen-notes.md]]

**Type:** article
**Confidence:** moderate

## Core Claim
A sharp knife and a claw grip make cutting faster and safer.

## Evidence
Curl the fingertips of the guiding hand under so the knuckles guide the blade. A dull knife slips and needs more force, which is when cuts happen.
