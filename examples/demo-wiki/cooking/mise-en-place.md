# Mise en Place

**Category:** cooking
**Sources:** [[RAW/kitchen-notes.md]]
**Updated:** 2026-01-15
**Type:** article
**Confidence:** moderate

## Core Claim
Preparing every ingredient before the heat goes on removes most cooking stress.

## Evidence
Read the recipe twice, measure and chop everything into small bowls, then start the stove. Fast dishes like stir fry punish anyone who chops while cooking.
