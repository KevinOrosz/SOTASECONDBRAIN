**Category:** learning
**Sources:** [[RAW/reading-notes.md]]
**Updated:** 2026-01-15
**Type:** article
**Confidence:** moderate

## Core Claim
Notes written in your own words are remembered better than copied text.

## Evidence
Summarise each idea in one sentence and link it to something you already know. Short linked notes are easier to revisit than long transcripts.
