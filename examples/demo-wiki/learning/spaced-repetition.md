# Spaced Repetition

**Category:** learning
**Sources:** [[RAW/study-guide.md]]
**Updated:** 2026-01-15
**Type:** article
**Confidence:** moderate

## Core Claim
Reviewing material at growing intervals beats cramming for long-term memory.

## Evidence
Each successful recall pushes the next review further out. Flashcard apps automate the schedule, but a paper box of index cards works too.
