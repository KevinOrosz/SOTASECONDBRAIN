# Watering

**Category:** gardening
**Sources:** [[RAW/garden-journal.md]]
**Updated:** 2026-01-15
**Type:** article
**Confidence:** moderate

## Core Claim
Deep, infrequent watering trains roots to grow down instead of staying shallow.

## Evidence
Water early in the morning at the base of the plant. A finger pushed into the soil up to the second knuckle tells you whether the root zone is still moist.
