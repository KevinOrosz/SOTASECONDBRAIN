**Category:** gardening
**Sources:** [[RAW/soil-notes.md]]
**Updated:** 2026-01-15
**Type:** article
**Confidence:** moderate

## Core Claim
Living soil with organic matter grows healthier plants than fertilizer alone.

## Evidence
Compost adds organic matter, feeds earthworms and fungi, and holds water like a sponge. A handful of crumbly dark soil that smells sweet is the simplest field test.
