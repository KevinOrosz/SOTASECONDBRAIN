# Companion Planting

**Category:** gardening
**Sources:** [[RAW/garden-journal.md]]
**Updated:** 2026-01-15
**Type:** article
**Confidence:** moderate

## Core Claim
Some plants protect or feed their neighbours when grown side by side.

## Evidence
Basil near tomatoes, beans fixing nitrogen for corn, and marigolds drawing pests away are classic pairings. Treat folk pairings as experiments and keep notes.
