# Composting

**Category:** gardening
**Sources:** [[RAW/soil-notes.md]]
**Updated:** 2026-01-15
**Type:** article
**Confidence:** moderate

## Core Claim
A balanced mix of green and brown scraps turns kitchen waste into soil food in weeks.

## Evidence
Aim for roughly two parts dry brown material such as leaves to one part green material such as vegetable peels. Turning the pile weekly keeps it aerobic and stops the smell.
